# Voice of Customer: Real Language from Pet Photographers Collective

This file contains direct quotes and language patterns from community members, organised by theme. Use these to ensure content sounds like it's written FOR these people, using language they actually use.

**How to use this file:** When creating content, scan the relevant theme sections to pick up natural phrasing, emotional tone, and specific words your audience uses. Mirror their language back to them in hooks, body copy, and calls to action.

---

## Theme 1: Inconsistent Bookings and Leads

**Direct quotes:**
- "Consistent leads and bookings."
- "Getting butts IN THE DOOR. My overhead is high and the economy is challenging."
- "I don't have inquiries I need to vet (nor a ton of inquiries incoming)."
- "My last 3 clients were people that I knew and one was a repeat client."
- "I get very few leads. Of those cold leads, maybe 20% will actually book a phone call."
- "Without a special project or promotion on the go, I get very few leads."
- "I do get occasional referrals, people who met me at events years ago, Google traffic, etc, but it's random and unpredictable."
- "No bookings at the moment - not sure if we're all thinking about the 3 day cold snap."
- "Getting my schedule booked (4-5 sessions a month). Ideally I'd like to be booking a month out due to demand."

**Language patterns:** "consistent bookings", "getting leads", "booking out", "inquiries coming in", "random and unpredictable", "getting people in the door"

## Common Lead Sources Mentioned

Understanding where their clients actually come from helps create relevant content:

- **Word of mouth / referrals** (most common but unpredictable)
- **Google / organic search** (valued highly when it works)
- **Instagram** (used by many but results vary widely)
- **Charity auctions / event vouchers** (common but often attract low-spend clients)
- **Facebook competitions / giveaways** (mixed results, often wrong-fit clients)
- **Returning clients** (reliable but not scalable)
- **Local events and networking** (effective when done strategically)
- **Email list** (underutilised by most members)
- **Book projects** (effective for some but project-dependent)

## Key Reframes They Respond To

These reframes from Ina consistently get engagement and "aha" moments:

- Selling is service, not pushing
- "It's not a sales problem, it's a positioning problem"
- "If someone only wants digitals and never enquires because of your messaging, that's your messaging doing its job"
- "Imperfect action is better than no action"
- The 3 gaps framework: is it visibility, message, or conversion?
- "Work less, earn more" by attracting ideal clients
- "Your messaging should warm them up BEFORE they enquire"
- AI can't replicate the experience and connection
- "When you say 'you're not great at selling' that's a mindset thing"
